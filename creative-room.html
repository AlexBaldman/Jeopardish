01 / Preservation

The memory does most of the work.

Keep the tall, formal, immediately recognizable game-show rhythm. Familiarity is the setup and should occupy nearly the entire logo.

02 / One move

Do exactly one strange thing.

The inserted O is the only visual rebel. When every letter performs, no letter is funny; when one defects, the joke becomes immediate.

03 / Timing

Let the audience see the transformation.

Motion begins as JEOPARDY!, then the O drops between R and D and nudges DY! aside. The logo animation itself tells the joke.

04 / Ownability

Build a parody, not a counterfeit.

The homage must be obvious, but our final lettering, color behavior, inserted O, and motion need enough authorship to belong to this universe.

# JeoPARODY Brand Identity Exploration v3

**Status:** Direction reopened; working hypothesis implemented for comparison  
**Working direction:** The Interloper  
**Date:** 2026-07-30

## The Correct Premise

The identity begins with a single spelling joke:

`JEOPARDY!` + one inserted `O` between `R` and `D` = `JEOPARODY!`

The recognizable game-show wordmark is the setup. The inserted O is the punchline.
Nothing else in the logo should compete with that transformation.

The original O in `JEO` is ordinary and stable. The inserted O after `R` is the only
mutable letter.

## Imagined Design Council

This is a synthesis of enduring identity-design principles, not a claim of endorsement
or an attempt to reproduce any named designer's exact work.

| Design lens | Useful question | JeoPARODY decision |
|---|---|---|
| Reduction | What is the one memorable move? | Insert one visibly foreign O |
| Continuity | What existing memory should remain untouched? | The tall, formal game-show wordmark rhythm |
| Visual wit | Can the viewer discover the joke without explanation? | PARODY appears inside the altered spelling |
| Motion titles | Can time make the idea clearer? | Begin as JEOPARDY!, then drop the O into the R-D gap |
| System design | Can one idea scale without becoming repetitive? | Keep the slot fixed; allow controlled O costumes |
| Restraint | What should not perform? | Every inherited letter |

## Four Directions

### 1. The Interloper

The base letters are formal, condensed, blue-white, and calm. The extra O is bright,
slightly crooked, ink-outlined, and visibly from another visual world.

**Strengths**

- The joke reads immediately.
- The original reference remains recognizable.
- The O can carry the game's comic and illustrated personality.
- Static and motion versions share the same idea.

**Risk:** If the O becomes too large or detailed, the title becomes a mascot lockup
rather than a wordmark.

**Current recommendation:** strongest working hypothesis.

### 2. The Undercover O

The inserted O nearly matches the inherited typography, with only an offset edge,
misregistration, or suspicious color exposing it as a forgery.

**Strength:** sophisticated and highly legible.

**Risk:** the punchline may be too quiet at small sizes or in a fast game header.

### 3. The Impact O

The O physically collides with the title, throwing sparks or nudging `DY!` to the right.

**Strength:** excellent title sequence and arcade attract-mode behavior.

**Risk:** too energetic for the everyday static master.

**Recommendation:** use as motion behavior around The Interloper, not as a separate
permanent wordmark.

### 4. The O of the Day

The inserted O becomes a donut, eye, coin, portal, eclipse, disco ball, or
episode-specific circular object.

**Strength:** supports episodes, collectibles, seasonal art, and story clues.

**Risk:** endless variants before a default is famous weaken recognition.

**Recommendation:** launch with one default Interloper O. Earn the costume system later.

## Working Logo Architecture

### Master

`[JEOPAR] + [INSERTED O] + [DY!]`

- Base letters: one visual voice, one color family, one calm rhythm.
- Inserted O: one contrasting color, a small rotation, and a clear circular counter.
- Exclamation point: inherited punctuation, not another mascot.

### Compact

Do not assume the O alone is distinctive enough. Test an `R-O-D` compact crop so the
insertion remains visible in context.

### One Color

The inserted O may use a slight rotation, outline, or registration offset, but the word
must remain readable without color.

## Motion Grammar

The logo animation is a three-beat joke:

1. **Setup:** `JEOPARDY!` appears completely straight.
2. **Interruption:** an O falls into the gap between R and D and nudges `DY!` right.
3. **Payoff:** `JEOPARODY!` holds long enough for the altered spelling to register.

Target duration: 900–1200 milliseconds. Reduced-motion mode displays the settled mark
immediately.

## Acceptance Tests

1. A new viewer reads `JEOPARODY!` in under one second.
2. A returning viewer recognizes the game-show homage before inspecting details.
3. The inserted O is unambiguously between R and D.
4. No special treatment is applied to the original O in JEO.
5. The logo remains readable at a 140-pixel header width.
6. The static mark works without animation.
7. The one-color mark still communicates an insertion.
8. The final custom lettering is transformative enough to belong to JeoPARODY rather
   than functioning as a counterfeit show logo.

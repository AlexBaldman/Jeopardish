## Former Executive Decision

The JeoPARODY identity is not a rainbow wordmark with a novelty object dropped into it.
It is a disciplined two-O system:

`JE + [CHANNEL O] + PAR + [UNAUTHORIZED O] + DY`

- The first O is the **living station ident**. It may become a portal eye, witness eye,
  donut, counterfeit coin, eclipse, disco signal, or episode-specific circular artifact.
- The second O is the **fixed evidence of the parody**. It is the added letter that turns
  the familiar setup into the word PARODY. It remains gold and stable.
- `JE` carries broadcast authority.
- `PARODY` carries the comic promise.

The logo's long-term value comes from recognition plus controlled mutation. The wordmark
must remain recognizable even when Channel O changes costume.

## Imagined Design Council

This synthesis uses useful design lenses associated with major identity traditions. It
does not claim endorsement by, or imitate the exact work of, any named designer.

| Lens | Question | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Reduction | What survives when everything decorative is removed? | Two O's, one mutable and one fixed |
| Narrative symbol | Can one shape tell the premise without explanatory copy? | Channel O is a breach, witness, and broadcast portal |
| Systems design | Can the identity work from favicon to arcade marquee? | Master, compact, mono, motion, and episode variants share geometry |
| Expressive typography | Where may the joke become loud? | PARODY carries energy; JE stays authoritative |
| Editorial wit | Can the identity reward a second look? | The fixed gold O is the unauthorized insertion |
| Pixel clarity | Does the symbol survive at 16 to 32 pixels? | Thick circular rim, dark center, one bright iris |
| Game identity | Can it animate without delaying play? | One short signal acquisition, then calm |
| Satirical worldbuilding | Does the brand imply a larger institution? | Channel O behaves like a counterfeit television network |

## Brand Thesis

**JeoPARODY is a comedy-learning broadcast that treats knowledge as verified truth and
everything around it as suspicious theater.**

The identity should combine:

- game-show authority;
- boardwalk arcade immediacy;
- graphic-novel inking;
- editorial parody;
- a little regional television impropriety;
- enough restraint to become recognizable rather than merely busy.

## Logo Architecture

### 1. Master Wordmark

Use the complete JeoPARODY wordmark for:

- landing-page mastheads;
- cabinet marquees;
- title cards;
- trailers;
- posters;
- merchandise with sufficient width.

Hierarchy:

1. warm ivory `JE`;
2. mutable Channel O;
3. magenta `PAR`;
4. fixed gold O;
5. magenta `DY`;
6. optional cyan exclamation mark in entertainment contexts.

### 2. Compact Mark

Use the Channel O portal-eye alone for:

- favicon;
- app icon;
- profile avatar;
- loading indicator;
- controller badge;
- tiny responsive surfaces.

The compact mark must not change silhouette when its interior costume changes.

### 3. One-Color Mark

Use `JE○PARODY` as one solid shape for:

- embroidery;
- legal and partner documents;
- stamps;
- accessibility modes;
- photocopy and fax jokes that must also function as real documents.

The circular O remains open and the word must remain readable without gradients.

### 4. Wordmark Without Motion

Static media defaults to the portal-eye Channel O. Do not rely on animation to explain
the concept.

## Typography

The production logo requires custom vector lettering. The current browser prototype uses
available fonts only to test hierarchy.

### Production Lettering Brief

**JE**

- compact broadcast slab construction;
- warm ivory face with restrained gold depth;
- slightly more formal than the remaining letters;
- no direct tracing of an existing television wordmark.

**PARODY**

- custom chunky display sans with comic rhythm;
- tightly fitted but readable counters;
- irregular enough to feel performed, disciplined enough to merchandise;
- the fixed O is rounder and warmer than neighboring letters.

**Interface**

- pixel face for tiny labels and state readouts;
- condensed utility face for controls and explanations;
- readable serif for clues where historically and legally appropriate.

## Color System

| Role | Color | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Broadcast ivory | `#FFF0B8` | authority, heritage, premise |
| Channel cyan | `#46EFFF` | signal, navigation, curiosity |
| Parody magenta | `#FF5BBB` | comic interruption, reveal |
| Unauthorized gold | `#FFD84D` | the inserted O, stakes, commitment |
| Learning green | `#7EFFA3` | voice readiness, mastery, positive outcome |
| Cabinet ink | `#080914` | outline, structure, legibility |
| Signal violet | `#6554FF` | portal depth and after-hours atmosphere |

### Color Rule

Color communicates ownership. Do not assign every letter a different color in the final
master mark. The visual joke is stronger when the two O's and the authority/parody split
carry the hierarchy.

## Channel O Wardrobe

Every Channel O costume must pass these tests:

1. circular silhouette;
2. dark or clearly open center;
3. high-contrast outer rim;
4. recognizable at 24 pixels;
5. no effect on letter spacing;
6. no reliance on tiny text;
7. no costume that makes the title read as a different word.

Initial costumes:

- **Portal Eye:** default master identity and story signal.
- **Witness Eye:** mystery, evidence, and host slip-ups.
- **Donut:** comedy episodes and lighter packs.
- **Counterfeit Coin:** wagering, scores, and fake-prize episodes.
- **Eclipse:** finales, night broadcasts, and suspense.
- **Disco Signal:** music, culture, and celebration packs.

## Motion Grammar

The logo gets one performance, not a screen saver.

### Signal Acquisition

1. Channel O appears as a dark ring.
2. Cyan and magenta energy acquires the rim.
3. The iris or costume resolves.
4. The fixed gold O gives one brief confirmation glint.
5. The mark becomes still.

Target duration: 650 to 900 milliseconds.

### Interaction

- A user may activate Channel O to cycle costumes.
- The transition should feel like changing a broadcast source.
- Continuous rotation is acceptable in an internal concept room but not as the default
  production idle state.
- Reduced-motion mode changes instantly with no spin.

## Voice and Taglines

### Primary Tagline

**TRIVIA GOT WEIRD**

Short, useful, and visually compatible with a cabinet marquee. It states attitude rather
than explaining the product.

### Supporting Lines

- **Truth at the core. Theater everywhere else.**
- **The forbidden broadcast for curious people.**
- **Knowledge is legal. The network is not.**
- **Verified facts. Counterfeit stakes.**
- **The extra O has breached containment.**

Use one supporting line at a time. Do not stack jokes under the logo.

## Brand Behavior

JeoPARODY should feel:

- intelligent without academic stiffness;
- hilarious without treating the player as the joke;
- premium without becoming sterile;
- nostalgic without behaving like a museum;
- strange without obscuring the game;
- satirical without sacrificing factual trust.

It should not feel:

- like a generic neon casino;
- like an unlicensed television-show skin;
- like every 1980s reference was applied simultaneously;
- like generated imagery without editorial control;
- like the logo must explain the entire lore.

## Production Deliverables

1. Draw custom master wordmark geometry.
2. Draw compact Channel O in 16, 24, 32, 64, 128, and 512-pixel optical sizes.
3. Draw one-color positive and reverse marks.
4. Draw six Channel O costumes on identical geometry.
5. Produce horizontal, stacked, cabinet, and title-card lockups.
6. Produce light, dark, print, embroidery, and motion specifications.
7. Replace browser-font prototype lettering only after the vector master passes the same
   responsive and accessibility fixtures as the current mark.

## Acceptance Tests

- A new viewer reads `JeoPARODY` in under one second.
- The first O clearly occupies the O in JEO.
- The second O clearly remains inside PARODY.
- The mark works in one color.
- The compact mark remains recognizable at 16 pixels.
- Animation is optional and respects reduced motion.
- No public mark uses the old Jeopardish title image.
- No public mark depends on likeness-based host art.
